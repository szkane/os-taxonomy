💡 Central idea (suggested)

Counting one by one tells you exactly how many — and the last number you say is the answer.

🧭 Lines of inquiry (suggested)

  • What exactly is counting & cardinality?
  • How do “Counting forwards and backwards” and “Counting big collections in groups” connect?
  • How can we use counting & cardinality in our own lives?

🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🍪Counting forwards and backwards — Count forwards and backwards in steps of 3 from 0
  • 🍪Counting big collections in groups — Quantify collections of up to 120 objects by partitioning them into equal groups (twos, fives or tens) and skip counting, explaining why grouping makes counting faster …
📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is building counting confidence — learning to skip count by 3s and count forwards and backwards in tens from any starting number.
