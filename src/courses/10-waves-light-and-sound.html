📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is learning how light travels in straight lines and using this understanding to explain everyday phenomena like how we see things and why shadows match the shape of objects that cast them.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Light Travels in Straight LinesLight bends round corners: 'the light curves round the edge to get where it needs to go' — the tell is 'I can see light near a corner so it must bend'Next step: Do the align-the-holes demo so the failure is seen; then connect it to shadows — a shadow has the object's sharp outline precisely because light cannot bend round the object.

Try at home (predict–observe–explain)

Predict whether you will still see the torch through all three holes after the middle card is nudged sideways. Say why first. Then slide the card and explain what the result proves about how light travels.

Activity ideas

  • Fire a beam, block it, and discover light won't turn corners
  • Three holes in a line — predict, then break the alignment
  • Draw and use ray diagrams to predict a shadow
  • You can't see round corners
