📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is mastering complex addition and subtraction — solving multi-step problems with large numbers and decimals, and choosing the best strategies to work out challenging calculations.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Adding and subtracting (age 10+)Gives a running total (e.g. the post-March figure) as the final answer, or drops the April-costs stepNext step: Re-read the question; mark all steps on the bar so the last subtraction is not dropped.
Addition and subtraction strategies (age 10+)Aligns the numbers by their right-hand digits instead of by the decimal point, so 3.4 + 2.78 lines the 4 under the 8Next step: Insist on lining up the points and adding a placeholder zero (3.40); use the re-scaled base-ten bench so tenths sit under tenths.
