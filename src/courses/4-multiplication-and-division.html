Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Division as equal sharingshares unequally — makes two piles that aren't equal or hands 'a lot' to one teddy; equality isn't yet the constraintNext step: model round-robin dealing ('one for you, one for you'), then count both piles and compare so 'fair' is pinned to 'equal'

Activity ideas

  • Share-and-group the same pile
  • Fair-share check with teddies
  • Sort the stories: sharing or grouping?
  • Correct the unfair sharer
