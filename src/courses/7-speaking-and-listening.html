🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🗣️Engaging Listeners and Valuing Viewpoints — Gain, maintain and monitor the interest of listeners when speaking; consider and evaluate different viewpoints, attending to and building on others' contributions; begin …
  • 🗣️Reporting & Recounting — Report on a topic or recount an experience with organised facts and descriptive details, speaking clearly at an understandable pace
  • 🗣️Giving and following instructions — Use interaction skills to receive and give spoken instructions: listening for each step, asking to clarify, and giving clear, ordered instructions to others
