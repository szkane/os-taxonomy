🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🌱Feeling of not understanding — Notice the feeling of not understanding — recognise when something is confusing rather than reading or listening past it
  • 🌱Thinking Before Starting — Before starting something new, stop and think: what do I already know about this topic?
  • 🌱Planning a Task — Make a simple plan before starting a task: what do I need to do, and what should I do first?
