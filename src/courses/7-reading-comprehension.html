🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 📖Main Ideas & Note-Taking — Identify main ideas drawn from more than one paragraph and summarise them; retrieve and record information from non-fiction texts using notes, tables or other methods
  • 📖Inferring Characters' Feelings and Motives — Draw inferences from independently-read texts, such as inferring characters' feelings, thoughts and motives from their actions, and justifying inferences with evidence …
  • 📖Forms of Poetry and Performance — Recognise different forms of poetry (free verse, narrative poetry, haiku) and discuss their features; prepare poems and play scripts to read aloud and perform with …
  • 📖Themes and messages — Identify recurring themes (good vs evil, friendship, bravery) and conventions (once upon a time, moral at the end, hero's journey) across a wide range of books including …
  • 📖Why the author wrote it — Distinguish one's own point of view from that of the author of an informational text and identify the author's purpose
  • 📖Story Lessons and Morals — Determine the central message, lesson, or moral of a story, fable, or folktale, explaining what the story teaches the reader and supporting the interpretation with key …
  • 📖Text Features & Presentation — Identify how language choices, text structure and presentational features (illustrations, diagrams, bold print, layout) contribute to the overall meaning and effect of a …
  • 📖Words that show characters — Discuss characters and settings across a range of texts and identify how language — adjectives, verbs, dialogue and descriptive phrases — presents them in different ways
  • 📖Identifying a text’s intended audience — Identify who a text is intended for using textual and visual clues such as its topic, words, language features, pictures, layout and where the text appears
  • 📖How characters and places connect — Discuss how characters and settings are connected in literature by First Nations Australian, other Australian and world authors and illustrators — how a place shapes …
  • 📖Same topic, different texts — Identify how similar topics and information are presented in different types of texts, such as a poem, an information report, a website and a picture book about the same …
