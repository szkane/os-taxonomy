Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Early Maths VocabularyUses an ambiguous everyday word where a precise one exists — 'big' for tall or for 'more', 'by' for a definite position — and does not notice the ambiguityNext step: Model refining in the moment ('do you mean taller, or more blocks?') and have the CHILD choose the precise word; build informal→formal, don't just supply the answer.
