🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🏞️Animal Life Cycles — Describe differences in the life cycles of mammals, amphibians, insects, and birds, comparing metamorphosis with direct development
  • 🏞️Plant & Animal Reproduction — Describe the life process of reproduction in some plants and animals, including sexual and asexual reproduction in plants
  • 🏞️Communities Protecting Resources — Obtain and combine information about ways individual communities use science ideas to protect the Earth's resources and environment
📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is learning about how living things reproduce and grow — comparing different life cycles from mammals to insects, and understanding the various ways plants and animals create new generations.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Animal Life Cycles'The caterpillar and butterfly are different animals — the caterpillar died and a new creature came out.'Next step: Open up what happens in the chrysalis: the same body is rebuilt, not replaced; trace continuity of the one individual through the stages.
Plant & Animal Reproduction'It's not reproduction without a seed / two parents — the plant is just growing bigger.'Next step: Define reproduction as producing a new individual; the runner plant is separate and complete — that is reproduction, just the asexual kind.
Communities Protecting ResourcesSlogan without mechanism — 'solar panels help because they're clean and green'Next step: Ask the follow-up 'where does the electricity come from if there are NO panels?' to force the fossil-fuel comparison that supplies the mechanism.

Activity ideas

  • Build and run four life cycles side by side
  • Explore labelled cycle diagrams and compare
  • Classify and sequence cycles
  • Talk it through: same creature or different animal?
