🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🧱Reading and writing numbers to 20 — Read and write numerals from 0 to 20
  • 🧱The teen numbers — Understand that the teen numbers (11–19) are composed of ten ones and some further ones (early place value)
  • 🧱Ordering numbers to 20 — Name, represent and order numbers from 0 to at least 20 using physical and virtual materials (number cards, on-screen ten frames and number tracks) and numerals
  • 🧱Number Words to Twenty — Read and write number words from one to twenty
  • 🧱Reading and writing numbers to 100 — Read and write numerals from 0 to 100, and describe the different ways numbers are used in everyday life (to count, measure, put things in order or label them)
📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is building number foundations — learning to read and write numbers up to 100, understanding number words, and beginning to see how numbers are built from tens and ones.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

The teen numbersSees the teen number as a pile of ones with no ten inside: 'it's just 14 ones'Next step: Bundle ten counters together; now 14 is 'a ten and four'. Compose and decompose several teens (13 = 10 + 3, 17 = 10 + 7).
Number Words to TwentyGuesses the irregular word from its first letters/shape: reads 'twelve' as 'twenty' or 'two'Next step: Treat number words as sight words: build automatic recognition of the irregular ones (one, two, twelve, eleven) alongside their numerals.

Activity ideas

  • Numeral studio: trace, build the quantity, self-correct
  • Hear it, write it: addressing the teen transposition
  • Read-and-match reference wall and checks
  • Make a ten and the leftovers
