Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Calculating Simple ProbabilityWrites favourable-to-unfavourable (part-part) instead of favourable-over-total — e.g. 3/7 for 3 blue among 10Next step: Recount the whole set aloud including the favourable outcomes; the total is everything that could be drawn, so 10 not 7 goes on the bottom
The 0-to-1 Probability ScaleCompares mixed notations by their surface digits without converting ('40 is biggest, then 4, then 0.3')Next step: Convert all three to a common form (decimals, or positions on the line) before comparing; show 1/4 = 0.25 sitting below 0.3
Probabilities Sum to OneIgnores the sum-to-1 constraint and assigns the missing outcome an arbitrary or 'equal' value by analogy ('green is 1/6, the same as blue')Next step: Add the given probabilities and show they don't yet reach 1; the missing value is whatever completes the whole, so subtract from 1 rather than copy another outcome
