🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🏎️Pushes & Pulls — Understand that pushes and pulls are forces that can change the speed or direction of an object's motion, and compare the effects of different strengths and directions
  • 🏎️Forces Vocabulary — Name and describe forces using precise vocabulary — force, push, pull, twist, stretch, squash, contact force, non-contact force, gravity, weight, friction, air …
  • 🏎️Shape and material change movement — Describe how objects move (roll, slide, bounce, spin) and how an object's own size, shape or material influences the way it moves
  • 🏎️Testing Push & Pull Designs — Analyse data to determine if a design solution works as intended to change the speed or direction of an object with a push or pull
📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is learning about forces — understanding how pushes and pulls can make objects move faster, slower, or change direction, and testing different ways to affect motion.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Pushes & Pulls'How hard you push doesn't change anything — a little tap and a big shove make it roll the same.'Next step: Line up two identical balls, tap one gently and shove the other, and race them so the child SEES strength map to speed, then name it.
Forces Vocabulary'The ball just falls because it's heavy' — doesn't name gravity or weight.Next step: Name gravity as the pull of the Earth and weight as that pull; use it whenever something falls.
Testing Push & Pull Designs'It didn't work, so I'll just try again the same way.'Next step: Ask 'did it go too far or not far enough?' then 'what could make it go a bit less/more?' — tie the change to the observation.

Try at home (predict–observe–explain)

Give one ball the same push on a smooth floor and on a rough carpet. Before rolling, ask the child to predict which travels further and WHY. …

Activity ideas

  • Flick-and-push play: feel a bigger push make a bigger change
  • Compare smooth vs rough: why things stop
  • Pushes slow and turn, not just start
  • Name pushes and pulls around us
