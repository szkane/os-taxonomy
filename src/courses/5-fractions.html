📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is discovering their first fractions — learning to recognise and name halves and quarters as equal parts of shapes, objects, and groups.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

What Is a Half?Any two pieces are halves - 'there are two bits, so they're halves' - the equal-size requirement is missingNext step: Cut two identical wholes, one fairly and one unfairly, and overlay the parts to show only equal parts match; use fair-share language ('is that fair?').
Finding halves and quarters (age 5+)Any four pieces are quarters - 'there are four pieces, so they're quarters' - equal-size ignoredNext step: Fold two identical squares, one into equal quarters and one unequally, and overlay to show only equal parts match.

Activity ideas

  • The halving bench: make it fair
  • Share the set first
  • Halved or not? Sort and choose
  • Reference display: name the parts
