🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🦋Human Life Stages — Describe the changes as humans develop to old age, including the stages of the human life cycle
  • 🦋Senses, Brain & Responses — Use a model to describe that animals receive information through their senses, process it in their brain, and respond in different ways
  • 🦋Structures for Survival — Construct an argument that plants and animals have internal and external structures that function to support survival, growth, behaviour, and reproduction
  • 🦋Organ Systems Vocabulary — Use technical vocabulary for the major organ systems — organ, organ system, circulatory system, digestive system, respiratory system, skeletal system, muscular system, …
📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is learning how plant and animal body parts help them survive and grow, while exploring the human life cycle and how our senses help us understand and respond to the world.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Human Life StagesEveryone changes at exactly the same age ('puberty happens on your 11th birthday')Next step: Show the normal range of puberty onset; stages are defined by changes, which start at different ages for different people.
Senses, Brain & ResponsesThe sense organ itself makes the body respond — the ears make the legs move, no brain neededNext step: Insert the brain into the model: the ear only detects; the brain receives, interprets and decides the response. Draw the three-box pathway.
Structures for Survivalstructures exist because the plant 'wants' or 'tries' to survive (teleology)Next step: reframe to how the structure functions, not intention; the feature works whether or not the plant 'tries'
Organ Systems Vocabularyswaps 'artery' and 'vein' ('veins carry blood away from the heart')Next step: anchor with 'A for Artery, Away from the heart'; use in several sentences until it is secure

Try at home (predict–observe–explain)

Do the dropped-ruler reaction-time test: one child holds a ruler, the partner's fingers wait at the zero mark to catch it when it drops. Ask them to PREDICT whether they can catch it the instant it's released, and why. …
