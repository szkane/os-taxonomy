📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is exploring how materials behave — investigating how solid objects can be changed by bending, twisting, or stretching, and learning why different materials are chosen for different jobs.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Changing Shapes of SolidsChanging the shape changes the material — 'the squashed clay isn't clay any more', bent metal 'became something else'.Next step: Confront on the spot: it's the same clay, same weight, and can be rolled back to a ball; only the shape changed, not the material.

Activity ideas

  • Materials deformation bench: apply a force, then let go
  • Embody and define the four actions
  • Predict, then test, and sort by how materials respond
  • Same material, new shape: show the stuff is unchanged
