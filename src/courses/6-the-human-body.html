Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Balanced Diet & Food GroupsVitamins give you energy and build muscle ('oranges make you strong').Next step: Separate the jobs: energy comes from carbohydrates, growth from protein; vitamins keep the body working and protect it — useful, but not fuel. Sort foods by the job they do.

  • Myth-busting: is this food bad for me?
