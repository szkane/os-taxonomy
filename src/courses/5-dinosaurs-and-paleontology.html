🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🦕Dinosaurs Were Real — Understand that dinosaurs were real animals that lived on Earth a very long time ago and are now extinct — none are alive today
  • 🦕Fossils & Palaeontologists — Understand that fossils are the remains of ancient living things preserved in rock, and that scientists called palaeontologists study fossils to learn about dinosaurs
  • 🦕Famous Dinosaur Species — Recognise and name common well-known dinosaur species: Tyrannosaurus rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, and Brachiosaurus/Diplodocus, describing a basic feature of each
  • 🦕Plant-Eaters vs Meat-Eaters — Sort dinosaurs into plant-eaters (herbivores) and meat-eaters (carnivores) by looking at clues like tooth shape — flat teeth for plants, sharp teeth for meat

📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is exploring the fascinating world of dinosaurs — learning about different species, understanding what fossils tell us about these ancient creatures, and discovering how scientists study life from long ago.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Dinosaurs Were RealDinosaurs are make-believe, like dragons and monsters.Next step: Show that fossils are real evidence — bones and footprints in rock you can touch in a museum; contrast with dragons, which leave none.
Fossils & PalaeontologistsA fossil must be a bone — a footprint doesn't count.Next step: Broaden the definition to traces: footprints, dung and burrows are fossils too, because they are preserved evidence of a living thing.
Famous Dinosaur SpeciesEvery big or scary dinosaur is a T. rex.Next step: Contrast signature features side by side — long neck versus tiny arms and big head; match each name to its stand-out feature.
Plant-Eaters vs Meat-EatersBig or scary-looking dinosaurs ate meat — the giant one must be a carnivore.Next step: Redirect to teeth: the biggest sauropods had blunt grinding teeth and ate plants; size doesn't decide diet.
Dinosaur SizesAll dinosaurs were gigantic.Next step: Show the range with paired examples — a house-tall sauropod beside a chicken-sized Compsognathus; size varied enormously.
Real Dinosaurs vs FictionAny big prehistoric reptile is a dinosaur — flying and swimming ones included.Next step: Draw the boundary: pterosaurs and plesiosaurs are relatives that lived alongside dinosaurs but aren't dinosaurs; dinosaurs were land animals of a specific group.

Activity ideas

  • Time ribbon: place the markers and find the gap
  • Two sorters: alive-today vs only-fossils, and real vs pretend
  • Museum-hall scene: bones, time signpost, and 'is it alive today?'
  • Talk it through: diagnostic and deeper probes
