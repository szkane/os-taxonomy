🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🏪Ethics in Business — Being honest, being fair, treating workers well, not harming the environment; what makes a 'good' business; whether businesses should care about more than profit
  • 🏪Supply Chains — Where products come from before they reach the customer; raw materials, making, transporting, and selling; the journey of a product from start to finish
  • 🏪Scaling Up — What happens when a small business grows; making more products, hiring people, reaching more customers; the challenges and opportunities of scaling
  • 🏪Pitching an Idea — Presenting a business idea to others convincingly; explaining what makes it good and why people should support it; building confidence in public speaking about ideas
