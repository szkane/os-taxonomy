🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🌍Seasonal changes — Observe changes across the four seasons and describe weather associated with each season, including how day length varies
  • 🌍Local weather patterns — Use and share observations of local weather conditions to describe patterns over time, recording temperature, rainfall, and other conditions
  • 🌍How Organisms Shape Habitats — Construct an argument supported by evidence for how plants and animals can change the environment to meet their needs
📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is learning to observe and describe weather patterns and seasons, while exploring how plants and animals change their environment to meet their needs.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Seasonal changes'It's winter/summer because today is cold/hot' — reads one day's weather as the seasonNext step: A season is a time of year with a pattern of weather, not one day — look at trees, daylight and animals too.
Local weather patterns'The weather is the same every day in a season' — doesn't expect day-to-day variationNext step: Within a season days still differ — that's why we record and look for patterns.
How Organisms Shape Habitats'Only people change the environment; animals just live in it'Next step: Give examples — beaver dam, bird nest, roots cracking a pavement.

Activity ideas

  • Tap-around year wheel: one season at a time
  • Spin the year and sort the clues
  • Ongoing real-world observation
  • Short spoken check: name the season and read the clues
