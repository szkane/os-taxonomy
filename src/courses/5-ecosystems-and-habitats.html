Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Plants and animals in their habitats'It would just learn to live there' — expects the organism to adapt to any place within its own lifetime.Next step: Go back to what living things need to survive (prerequisite); a place only works if it supplies those needs — swapping them removes the need.
Reducing Human ImpactRubbish vanishes — 'it's fine, the rain or wind will wash it away' or 'it just rots and disappears'Next step: Confront the persistence of waste: ask what a plastic bottle looks like after a whole year outside; establish that most litter stays and builds up.

Activity ideas

  • Connect the needs model: link each need to what the place provides
  • Tap-to-reveal habitat reference: 'this place gives me...'
  • Match organisms to habitats with a reason, and spot the missing need
  • Talking-animal prompt: 'where should I live, and why?'
