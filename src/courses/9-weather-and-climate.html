📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is exploring how the Sun drives weather patterns and creates different climate zones around Earth, learning about extreme weather events, climate change, and how people design solutions to protect communities from weather hazards.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Climate Change Basics'Global warming is caused by the hole in the ozone layer letting extra heat in' — conflates ozone depletion with the greenhouse effectNext step: Separate the two: ozone hole = more UV over a region; greenhouse gases = trapped outgoing heat everywhere.
Sun-Driven Weather Systems'Land and water heat up at the same rate'Next step: Measure sand vs water under a lamp — sand heats faster and cools faster.
Climate Zones'The equator is hot because it's the closest part of Earth to the Sun'Next step: Shine a torch straight-on vs slanted onto a globe — slanted light spreads the same heat over more area.
Natural resources'Renewable means it makes electricity; non-renewable means it doesn't'Next step: Renewable is about the resource being naturally replaced, not about what it powers.
Weather-Resistant Engineering'A good enough design stops the weather event / makes you completely safe'Next step: Every defence has a limit (barriers overtop, shelters need warning time) — name one.
Extreme Weather Events'Hurricanes and tornadoes are the same thing, just different names'Next step: Contrast size, lifespan and where each forms (ocean vs land).

Try at home (predict–observe–explain)

Put a tray of dry sand and a tray of water side by side under a desk lamp. Ask the child to PREDICT which will warm up faster, and once the lamp is switched off, which will stay warm longer — with reasons — before measuring both with thermometers. …
