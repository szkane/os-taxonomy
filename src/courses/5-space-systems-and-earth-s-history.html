🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 🪐Why seasons change — Make observations at different times of year to relate the amount of daylight to the time of year, noticing longer days in summer and shorter days in winter
  • 🪐Naming the Planets — Name the planets in our solar system in order from the Sun and use vocabulary for space — planet, star, sun, moon, satellite, orbit, solar system, galaxy, universe, …
📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is discovering how daylight changes throughout the year by observing that days are longer in summer and shorter in winter.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Why seasons change'summer has more daylight because it's hotter' — treats warmth as the cause of day lengthNext step: Separate 'how long it stays light' from 'how warm it is' — chart sunset time and temperature side by side; they travel together but the question is about hours of light, not heat.
Naming the Planetstreats the Sun as a planet — 'the Sun is the biggest planet' — because it is a big ball in spaceNext step: Contrast light-MAKER (star) with light-BORROWER (planet): the Sun makes its own light, planets only reflect it; sort a few objects by that test.

Activity ideas

  • A year in a slider: watch the daylight grow and shrink
  • Winter morning vs summer evening: two scenes side by side
  • Record the change yourself over real weeks
  • Check and sort: season to more-or-less daylight
