Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

Growing Up & Pubertyyou grow and change because you eat enough food — 'more food makes puberty happen'Next step: separate raw materials (food supplies the body) from the trigger/signal (hormones start the changes)
Healthy Lifestyle Choiceshealth is about weight and looks — 'if you're slim and feel fine, you're healthy'Next step: separate outward appearance from internal system health; ask what the heart, arteries and teeth are doing out of sight
