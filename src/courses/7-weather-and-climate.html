📖 Goals, common misconceptions & printable ideas

Learning goal

Your child is learning about weather science — how meteorologists forecast weather, what causes different weather patterns like wind and storms, and understanding the water cycle that brings us rain and snow.

Common misconceptions (from teaching research)

The Water Cycle'The water just dried up and it's gone' — treats the puddle disappearing as the water being destroyedNext step: Catch the vapour: a covered dish shows the water reappears as droplets — hidden, not gone.
Geography & Local Weather'The only thing that changes weather is how far north you are' — single-factor thinkingNext step: Compare paired places that differ in only ONE factor at a time.
Weather vs Climate'It was hot/cold today, so climate change is/isn't real' — treats one day's weather as proof about climateNext step: Put one day beside a 30-year average graph — show one point can't reveal the trend.
Cloud Types'Clouds are made of smoke / cotton wool / fog stuff'Next step: Link kettle-steam and breath-on-cold-glass to droplets condensing from vapour.
What Causes Wind'Trees and waves make the wind by swishing about'Next step: Reverse it — trees move BECAUSE of wind; wind blows over open sea with no trees at all.
Weather Forecasting'The forecaster is wrong whenever it doesn't happen exactly' — reads a probability as a promiseNext step: Unpack the %: 70% means about 3 in 10 such days stay dry — a dry day is expected sometimes.

Try at home (predict–observe–explain)
