💡 Central idea (suggested)

Good readers THINK while they read: they predict, question, picture, connect and check — on real stories, at the moment meaning goes fuzzy.

🎯 What you’ll learn

  • 💭Reviewing Own Writing — Evaluate whether your own writing achieves the effect you intended on a reader — go beyond checking for correctness to asking whether it actually works
  • 💭Knowing What You Don't Know — Monitor your own vocabulary gaps — notice words you half-know, distinguish confident from uncertain knowledge, and develop strategies to resolve the uncertainty
