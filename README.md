# PYP Marble Courses — an AI-generated IB PYP practice-site DEMO

> **This is a DEMO, not a curriculum.** The [`src/`](src/) folder is a fully
> playable online inquiry and practice site for primary school children aged
> **5–10 (Kindergarten to Grade 5 / K–G5)**, generated by AI coding on top of the
> [Marble Skill Taxonomy](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy),
> the [`/marble-builder`](.agents/skills/marble-builder/SKILL.md) skill, and the
> [ib-pyp-html5-generator](https://github.com/szkane/ib-pyp-html5-generator)
> skill. It exists to show what is now possible — and to invite parents and
> teachers to build **their own** personalized version for their child.
>
> 🇨🇳 中文说明见 [README.zh-CN.md](README.zh-CN.md)

> **About this fork:** the upstream repository
> ([withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy))
> is the open **Marble Skill Taxonomy** — a structured graph of what children
> learn: 1,838 micro-topics, 3,878 prerequisite edges, 9 subjects,
> curriculum-standard alignments, and teaching research. That core data is
> preserved; everything in [`src/`](src/) and [`tools/`](tools/) is created to
> bring the taxonomy to life as an inquiry-based web learning experience.

## What you are looking at

A static site (`src/`) with **304 playable primary-school course pages** across
**66 domains** — strictly aligned to primary school (Ages 5–10 / K–G5) with
**zero cross-grade content leakage**. Each course wraps interactive learning in
an IB PYP inquiry framework:

- **100% Explore-First inquiry opener** — every single course opens with a visual
  simulation (`phase: 'explore'`) before direct instruction or testing: an
  interactive Canvas/Konva 2D lab, a Three.js 3D world, or an illustrated
  reading strategy book.
- **Two-step `🔭 Explore → Challenge` flow in every game engine** — discovery
  always comes first:
  - *Concept labs & 3D worlds* start in free-play `[🔭 Explore]` with commits hidden.
  - *Category sorters* provide `[🔭 Explore items]` with an Inquiry Spotlight card before sorting.
  - *Timelines & cycles* offer a step-by-step `[🔭 Explore stages]` walkthrough before ordering.
  - *Sentence clinics* have an interactive `[🔭 Explore rules]` bandage demo before diagnosis.
  - *Dilemma quests* include a `[🔭 Explore meters]` sandbox before multi-scene decision quests.
  - *Memory matching* presents `[🔭 Meet the pairs]` face-up with audio before playing.
  - *Word studios* feature `[🔭 Explore words]` phonics & morpheme tiles before letter building.
- **Inquiry Wonder Cards & POE Reflection** — `flashcards` are framed as
  **Inquiry Wonder Cards** (`🤔 Wonder` → `💡 Discovery` + `🔬 Try noticing`);
  `mission` provides Predict-Observe-Explain (POE) questions with concept focus
  badges, researched misconception distractors, and an **IB Learner Profile
  Reflection** selector (`Inquirer`, `Thinker`, `Reflective`, `Communicator`)
  at completion.
- **18 game engines over 32 animated simulation scenes**:
  - **12 2D engines**: `concept-lab` (with eased dials and ambient particle layers),
    `sorter`, `sequence`, `decision`, `doctor`, `flashcards`, `arcade`, `memory`,
    `word-builder`, `worksheet`, `storybook` (with Rough.js and 58 canvas-drawn actors),
    and `mission`.
  - **6 Three.js 3D worlds**: cel-shaded Earth rotation (`spatial3d`), 3D Solar System
    tour (`solar3d`), tectonic volcano pressure lab (`volcano3d`), 3D Solid Geometry &
    Polyhedra Inspector (`geometry3d`), 3D Light, Shadow & Material Optics Studio
    (`optics3d`), and 3D Layered World Diorama (`diorama3d` across Earth crust, ocean zones,
    biomes, fossil rock strata, landforms, and historical civilizations).
  - **32 Canvas 2D scenes in `scenes.js`**: double ten-frames, number lines (to 100 / 1,000 / 10,000),
    fraction line aligned to decimals & percentages, base-10 blocks (H/T/O and Th/H/T/O), 7-digit
    place-value charts, 2D coordinate grids & protractors, synchronized analog + 24h digital clocks
    with elapsed time, dual capacity jugs + 3D cubic volume cubes, water cycle, food webs, heart & lungs,
    controlled fair-test benches, robot code turtles, 4-bit binary switches, historical time dials,
    budget scales, and mindful breathing gardens.
- **Strict per-age spiral progression (Ages 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10)** — children play
  mechanics matched to their exact grade level with **zero cross-age borrowing**:
  an Age 5 child works with ten-frames and apple counters within 10, an Age 7 child hops
  a number line in hundreds, and an Age 10 child explores 4-digit place values and 3D cubic volume.
- **Child-first, accessible UX** — children see only the learning stage; UOI
  transdisciplinary units and research notes live in a toggleable "Grown-ups"
  panel. Features read-aloud TTS with follow-along word highlighting, dashed-underline
  vocabulary popovers, no answer leaks, dark/light theme contrast rules, no accounts,
  no tracking, and full offline capability.

## Try it

```bash
npm run build:site             # regenerates src/ from data/ and runs validate-content.mjs
node tools/audit/age-fit.mjs   # audits band & per-age curriculum fit across all 66 domains
npm run serve:site             # serves src/ on http://127.0.0.1:4173
```

Open the hub (`src/index.html`), filter by subject or grade (`K, G1, G2, G3, G4, G5`),
and play. The build is self-validating:

- `tools/validate-content.mjs` verifies every authored sentence, quiz bank, dial span,
  case reachability, and prompt length, ensuring zero pre-solved cases and zero identical
  content served across different ages.
- `tools/audit/age-fit.mjs` verifies representations against curriculum topics across all
  66 domains (currently reporting `Flag counts: {}` with 0 mismatches).

`src/` is pure static files: a GitHub Pages workflow (`.github/workflows/deploy-pages.yml`)
and Cloudflare Pages configuration (`wrangler.toml`) are ready out of the box.

## Where the content comes from

```
data/topics.json            1,838 micro-topics (age, domain, evidence, research)
data/clusters.json          176 curated course cluster summaries
data/teaching-research.json 1,049 teaching-research records
        │
        ▼
tools/lib/mapping.mjs        per-domain pedagogy: canonical representations,
                             CHILD_GLOSS vocabulary, and game configurations
tools/lib/band-modes.mjs     band-tiered interactive content (early / mid / upper)
tools/lib/lab-cases.mjs      band-specific lab challenge cases
tools/lib/age-curriculum.mjs per-age (5..10) simulation resolution & mode specialization
        ▼
tools/build-site.mjs         derives 304 static primary-school course pages:
                             inquiry framing, 18 engines, 32 scenes, Wonder Cards,
                             and POE mission checks with Learner Profile reflection
        ▼
tools/validate-content.mjs   machine-checks all child-facing content (build gate)
        ▼
src/                         static, offline-first, dependency-free learning site
```

The underlying taxonomy — **1,838 micro-topics, 3,878 prerequisite edges, 9 subjects** —
is **[withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy)**.
The page shell, IB PYP framing, and inquiry patterns come from
**[szkane/ib-pyp-html5-generator](https://github.com/szkane/ib-pyp-html5-generator)**
and the **marble-builder** skill.

## The actual point: build YOUR child's version

Every child's learning journey is unique, and no static demo can replace
personalized inquiry. The goal of this project is to prove that the ingredients
for personalized, high-quality tutoring now fit on a single computer:

1. **The learning graph.** The taxonomy decomposes primary education into
   fine-grained micro-topics with prerequisites, developmental age ranges, and
   observable mastery evidence ("I can…") — turning broad subjects into checkable,
   connected stepping stones.
2. **The generator.** The ib-pyp-html5-generator and marble-builder skills encode
   how to translate learning objectives into interactive simulations, 3D explorations,
   and inquiry challenges.
3. **AI coding.** This entire experience — from 32 Canvas simulation scenes and
   6 Three.js engines to the self-checking audit test suite — was built and iterated
   with agentic AI coding.

A suggested workflow for parents and educators:

- Start from the child's **real school evidence** — homework reflections, teacher
  feedback, or a concept that didn't quite click.
- Locate the concept in `data/topics.json`, and trace prerequisite edges in
  `data/dependencies.json` to find where the foundation needs strengthening.
- Prompt your AI assistant to generate a dedicated inquiry course using this repository's
  `tools/` and `src/assets/` architecture: an interactive visual simulation to explore first,
  hands-on challenges, Wonder Cards, and POE reflection.
- Observe how your child interacts — what sparks wonder, what builds confidence — and iterate.
